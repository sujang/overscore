# v1.3.9 Release Notes (for App Store submission)

```
• You can now pick a home team you've entered before, filling in both its name and team color at once. Tap "Choose Home from Past Teams" on the new match screen or the scoreboard settings screen

• Teams are added to the list automatically when you create a match or save scoreboard settings. Swipe to delete teams you no longer need

• Fixed an issue where the app could close when the screen auto-locked during a long video import. The screen no longer auto-locks while videos are being imported

• On iOS 26 or later, video import progress is now also shown on the Lock Screen and elsewhere in the system

• If the app closes while you are creating a new match, the team names and videos you have already loaded are now saved, and you can pick up where you left off from the match list the next time you open the app
```
